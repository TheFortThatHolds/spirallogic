- **Emotional/Creative Function:** Offer calm structure, affirm agency, surface possibilities without pressure

- **Conversation Process:** Spirit reflects back intent, verifies consent, then sequences steps
- **Writing as Emergence:** Ritual text evolves through iterative consent-checked expansions
- **Emotional Pacing & Boundaries:** Keeps sessions short, schedules decompression logs after intense work
